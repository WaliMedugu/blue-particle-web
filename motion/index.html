FAQs

Questions before you book

Why is the price a range?

Scope and stage. A seed-stage team with one clear feature sits at the low end. A Series A launch with more screens, more cut-downs and tighter dates sits at the high end. Your quote is fixed before we start.

How fast can you deliver?

Storyboard by day 3, first cut by day 7, final by day 10 from an approved brief. The Full system includes priority turnaround of 7 to 10 days for everything.

What do you need from us?

A link to the product, access to a demo or Figma file if you have one, your brand assets and the launch date. We write the script if you don't have one.

Can we run the film as a paid ad?

Yes. Music and fonts are licensed for commercial use, and you get the sizes each platform needs.

How do payments work?

50% deposit to start, 50% when you approve the watermarked final. We invoice in USD and accept international transfers.

Where are you based?

Abuja, Nigeria (GMT+1). We work async with teams in the US, UK and Europe, with calls in your hours when you need them.
