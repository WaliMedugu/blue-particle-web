    [ 05 · Questions ]

    Before you ask.

    How do I qualify for the student rate?

    Send a photo of your current student ID with your brief. It's for projects like final-year work, hackathons and campus startups.

    What counts as a startup?

    Pre-revenue or under two years old. If you're unsure, ask. We'd rather help than turn you away.

    How long does it take?

    Most single videos take about 5 to 7 days from an approved brief. Launch packs take a little longer. We give you the exact date in writing before you pay.

    How does payment work?

    50% to start, 50% when you approve the final cut. We accept Nigerian transfers and international payments.

    What do you need from me?

    A link to your app or site, your logo, and a few lines on who it's for. If you have screen recordings or brand colours, even better.

    What if I want more than two revisions?

    Extra rounds are available at a stated price. Nothing is billed without your yes.

    Can I use the video in ads?

    Yes. Music and fonts are licensed for commercial use, and you get every size you need for social and ads.
