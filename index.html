Questions

Before you book

Is the price fixed?

Yes. The price on the package is what you pay. If your video needs more than a package covers, we tell you before we start, and no job goes above ₦300,000.

How fast is it?

Plan by day 3, first cut by day 7, final by day 10. Short clips are often ready in 3 to 5 days.

What do you need from me?

What the video is for, your logo, any photos or videos you have, and the date you need it. We write the script if you don't have one.

Can I run it as a paid ad?

Yes. The music and fonts are licensed for ads, and you get the size each platform needs.

How do I pay?

Half to start, half when you approve the final video. Bank transfer in Naira. Clients outside Nigeria can pay the same price in USD.

Where are you?

Abuja, Nigeria. We work with people anywhere, over WhatsApp or email.

Got something to show?

Send us a message. Within a day you get an idea for your video and a fixed price.
