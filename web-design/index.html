FAQs

Questions before you build

What kinds of websites do you build?

Landing pages, business and portfolio sites, online stores with ordering, and web apps with logins and dashboards. Everything on this page is live.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes about a week. Larger sites and web apps are scoped in writing, with dates for design sign-off and launch.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, photos, the words you want on the site (we can write them) and any sites you like. That's enough to start.

Can you make a launch film for the site too?

Yes. That's the point of one studio: the film, the hero loop and the site share one look. See motion design.

How do payments work?

50% to start, 50% before the site goes live on your domain. We invoice in Naira or USD.
