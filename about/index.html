Questions

About BPS

What is BPS?

BPS is Blue Particle Studios, a motion design studio in Abuja, Nigeria. We make motion graphics, brand films, ads, launch videos and short-form edits, and we design and build websites.

Where is Blue Particle Studios based?

Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria. We work with clients across Nigeria and worldwide over WhatsApp and email.

What does Blue Particle Studios make?

Brand films, video ads, product and app launch films, event promos, TikTok and Instagram Reels edits, logo animation and kinetic typography, plus websites.

Who has Blue Particle Studios worked with?

Universities, brands, apps and events, including films for Nile University, Pan-Atlantic University, NOK Realty, EasyWay, Arguuu and Faaji Fashion Market, and concept films for Glovo and OpenAI.

How much does a motion design video cost at BPS?

Fixed prices from ₦75,000 for one video, ₦150,000 for a video with short cut-downs, and ₦300,000 for a full campaign. No job costs more than ₦300,000.

How fast does Blue Particle Studios deliver?

A plan by day 3, the first cut by day 7 and the final video by day 10. Short clips are often ready in 3 to 5 days.

How do I hire Blue Particle Studios?

Send a WhatsApp message to +234 906 195 0587 or email blueparticlestudios@gmail.com, or fill in the form at blueparticlestudios.com. You get an idea and a fixed price within a day.
