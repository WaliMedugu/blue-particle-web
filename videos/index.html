    [ 06 · Questions ]

    Before you ask.

    Do I need to have footage or a script?

    No. Send us what you have: photos, your logo, a menu, a few lines about what you do. We write the script and build the video from there.

    Do you film on location?

    Not at the moment. We animate and edit from your photos, logo, screenshots and footage. If you have clips from a phone or camera, we can work them in.

    How long does it take?

    A quick clip is usually 3 to 5 days, a business promo about a week, and a campaign a little longer. You get the exact date in writing before you pay.

    How does payment work?

    50% to start, 50% when you approve the final cut. We accept Nigerian transfers and international payments.

    Can the video be used for ads?

    Yes. Music and fonts are licensed for commercial use, and you get every size you need for social and ads.

    What if I need something different?

    Tell us. Custom quotes are free, and most projects can be shaped to fit a budget.

    I'm a student or a startup. Is there a cheaper option?

    Yes, there is a dedicated page with student and startup rates from ₦25,000. See startup & student pricing.
